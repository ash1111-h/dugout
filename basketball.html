How to play

You get a start player and a target player. Name a teammate of the start player, then a teammate of that player, and keep going until you reach someone who played with the target.

  • Teammates means they were on the same team's roster in the same season.
  • You have 6 guesses. A wrong guess uses one up and does not move your current player.
  • You cannot use the same successful player twice. A teammate of the target completes the chain; guessing the target directly works too.
  • Hints can be reused, but every use is counted on your share card.
  • The puzzle's standard links use players with 20+ games for that team-season. Shorter stints still count as guesses and are marked on the link.

Daily is the same puzzle for everyone and changes at midnight Pacific time. It gets harder through the week, from warm-ups early on to overtime on Sunday. Past puzzles are in the Puzzle menu and do not count toward streaks. Unlimited lets you pick a difficulty and keep a run going.

Optional email/password accounts let you sync daily progress and stats across devices. You can choose whether your display name and scores appear on the public leaderboard. Guests can play without signing in; guest and account saves are kept separately on shared devices.

Leaderboard

      Basketball data: seasons through 2016-17 come from the public “The NBA CSV” dataset (peasant98/TheNBACSV, from Basketball-Reference); seasons from 2017-18 on come from public NBA tracking tables (gabriel1200/site_Data). From 2017-18 on the source lists only a player's final team each season, so mid-season trades are missing from those years. Players are matched across sources by name and age, which can occasionally split or merge two people. Standard links use 20+ games for a team-season. Daily puzzles are generated deterministically from 2026-11-23. Seasons are labeled by the years they span, and “recent debut” means a first season ending in 2023 or later.
